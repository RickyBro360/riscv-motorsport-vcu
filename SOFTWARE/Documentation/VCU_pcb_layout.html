F1 VCU PCB Layout

A complete, routed layout for the VCU board, with every off-board part wired to the pin it belongs on. Every pad, resistor, capacitor and module header is drawn at its real position. The routing uses 45° traces on two copper layers and a ground pour. Hover over or tap any pad, trace or wire to follow its net through the board and out to the adapter, buck, L298N, sensors, servos, CAN bus, Pi and ground station.

Before you copy this into KiCad: the U1 pin numbers follow your schematic symbol, with pin 1 marked by the square pad, so make sure your footprint matches. Check the INA260 and CAN module header orders against your actual modules. The layout also includes the fixes from the review: servo headers are GND, +5V, PWM(, FB); the 5V_IN label is replaced by +5V; INA260 VBUS is tied to IN−; C4/C5 are added on the servo rail; and J15 has moved next to J14 so all the power enters at one edge.

Routing rules used

    Why it's placed this way

    • Power enters at one edge. J14 (12 V through the INA260), J15 (5 V from the buck) and J16 (motor PWM) sit together on the bottom edge, so the heavy wiring stays in one corner of the car.
    • No external reset circuit. Pin 37 (EN) is left unconnected. On this board it most likely enables the regulator, and the old R11/C3/SW1 circuit held it low so the board never powered up. The module's own RESET button does the job.
    • Bulk capacitor at the load. C4/C5 sit beside the two MG996R headers at the top, where the stall current is drawn.
    • Dividers near the ADC. R2/R3 sit by the servo headers, so the raw ~5 V wiper voltage never travels far. R7/R8 and the filter caps C1/C2 sit next to GPIO0/1.
    • Headers follow the pin order. The servo headers run left to right in the same order as GPIO21→18, so their PWM traces nest without crossing. Halls, the encoder and the feedback go on the left because their pins are on U1's left column. I²C, CAN, 1-Wire and the right-hand halls go on the right.
    • Solid ground. The bottom layer is almost all ground pour, and bottom traces are used only for crossings. Every GND pad is through-hole, so it lands on the pour directly.
    Placement and routing were generated by a two-layer grid router from the project's KiCad schematic (30 Sep 2026) with the review fixes applied. The off-board pinouts come from the Stage 2d firmware, raspberry_pi/main.py and ground_radio_bridge.ino. Treat it as a routing guide: run KiCad's DRC on your own copy before ordering.
